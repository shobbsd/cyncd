At a glance

  • We only collect your email address, and only if you join the waitlist.
  • We use it to tell you when cyncd launches. Nothing else.
  • We never sell it or share it for marketing.
  • Ask at any time and we’ll delete it.

01Who we are

This website is run by Cyncd Technology Ltd (“Cyncd”, “we”, “us”), a company registered in England and Wales under company number 17158462. Our registered office is 3rd Floor, 86–90 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NE, United Kingdom.

We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at contact@cyncd.co.uk.

02What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect the email address you enter. Like any website, our form provider also receives basic technical information when you submit, such as your IP address and browser type, which it uses to deliver the form and block spam.

We don’t ask for any other information, and this website doesn’t collect any health or cycle data.

03How we use it

  • To email you when cyncd launches, and possibly with a small number of updates about the launch.
  • To keep a record that you asked to join.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by joining. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any email from us or by writing to contact@cyncd.co.uk. Every email we send will include a way to opt out.

04Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers who handle your data on our behalf and only as we instruct:

FormspreeReceives and stores waitlist signups and forwards them to us by email. Based in the United States.
ZohoProvides our email service, so your address is held in our Zoho Mail inbox.
Google FontsDelivers the typeface on this website, and receives your IP address when the page loads.

Where a provider stores data outside the UK, we rely on the safeguards the law requires for international transfers, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

We will never sell your email address or give it to anyone for their own marketing.

05How long we keep it

We keep your email address until cyncd launches and we’ve let you know, or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first. If cyncd hasn’t launched within 24 months of you joining, we’ll delete the waitlist.

06Cookies

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking.

07Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • withdraw your consent at any time
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it

To use any of these rights, email contact@cyncd.co.uk. We’ll respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

08Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use your email address, we’ll tell you by email first.

Questions about your data?

We read every message and reply within one month.
