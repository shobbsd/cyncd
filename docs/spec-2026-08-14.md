# cyncd — Phase 1 spec

**Source:** `CYNCD meeting 14.08.2026.pdf`
**Written against:** the ported Expo app as of this document's commit.
**Status:** draft — the Open Questions at the end block parts of the build.

Notation: **[NOTES]** is taken from the meeting document, quoted or closely
paraphrased. **[REC]** is a recommendation from this document, not something
the meeting decided. Anything marked **[OPEN]** needs a decision before the
relevant work can start.

---

## 1. What changed

The meeting is a re-spec, not a revision list. Measured against what the app
does today:

- One entirely new screen (**Forecast/Track**, Her only).
- One entirely new cross-cutting feature (**cyncd Score**) that appears on four
  screens.
- Three of the four existing screens change their underlying model, not just
  their layout: **Partner** inverts from derived to user-controlled, **Plan**
  gains a calendar and an inline assistant, **Reflect** goes from a structured
  form to a conversation.
- Navigation becomes asymmetric between the two users for the first time.

Only **Today** survives largely intact, and it gains the Score preview.

## 2. Blocking conflict: the content lint

`src/content/content-rules.test.ts` currently forbids most of the vocabulary
this spec requires.

```ts
const BANNED = [
  'hormone', 'hormonal', 'menstrual', 'ovulat', 'luteal', 'follicular', 'pms',
  'symptom', 'diagnos', 'medical', 'clinical', 'therapy', 'treatment', 'disorder',
  'chart', 'graph', 'score',
  'streak', 'badge', 'you missed', "don't forget", 'keep it up',
]
```

plus a separate rule that `cycle` appears nowhere outside onboarding.

**[NOTES]** require `cyncd Score` (the headline feature), `Current menstrual
cycle phase`, `Cycle day`, and both `Physical symptoms` and `Emotional
symptoms` as tracker fields. Every one of those fails the lint today.

The rules are not simply wrong. They encode something the notes independently
insist on: *"the partner must not automatically see: her cycle day, her
menstrual phase, symptoms she has logged."* The rules are right about the
audience and wrong about the scope.

**[REC] Rescope the ban by audience rather than globally.**

| Surface | Clinical + cycle vocabulary |
|---|---|
| Her Forecast / Track (private) | **Allowed** — it is a tracker; refusing the words makes it useless |
| Today, Partner, Plan, Reflect, notifications, Score copy | **Banned** — unchanged from today |

Concretely:

1. Split `BANNED` into `BANNED_EVERYWHERE` and `BANNED_IN_SHARED`.
2. Remove `'score'` from the banned list entirely.
3. Add the notes' own prohibitions to `BANNED_EVERYWHERE`: `bad day`,
   `low compatibility`, `relationship risk`, `difficult phase`,
   `hormonal behaviour`, `she may be emotional`, `avoid her today`.
4. Replace the blanket `cycle` rule with one scoped to shared surfaces.

Until this is decided, none of the new content can be written, because the
test suite rejects it. **Do this first.**

## 3. cyncd Score

**[NOTES]** A daily percentage showing how naturally aligned the couple may
feel that day and how much intentional care may help them connect. Playful,
emotionally intelligent, shareable.

> It is not a compatibility test and it does not measure whether the
> relationship is good or bad.

### 3.1 Calculation

**[NOTES]** Phase 1 does not require advanced AI. Rule-based, from: her current
cycle day and predicted phase, predicted mood and emotional sensitivity,
predicted energy and social capacity, expected communication style, recent
accuracy feedback, feedback submitted separately by each partner, and
completion of the suggested shared action.

Suggested starting weights:

| Weight | Component |
|---|---|
| 40% | Predicted communication alignment |
| 30% | Emotional and energy compatibility |
| 20% | Shared action or check-in completion |
| 10% | Recent accuracy feedback |

**[NOTES]** *"This calculation can be adjusted technically if there is a clearer
or more reliable way to produce the same user experience."*

**[REC]** Implement as a pure function in `src/content/score.ts` with unit
tests per band. No model call — this is deterministic, cheap, and testable, and
keeping it that way is what makes it demonstrable.

### 3.2 Categories and language

**[NOTES]** Warm and supportive language, always:

| Band | Label | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 80–100% | Naturally aligned | Connection and communication may feel more natural today. |
| 60–79% | Gently connected | There is good potential for connection, but thoughtfulness may make the difference. |
| 40–59% | Different rhythms | Both users may need different things today, so clear communication will be important. |
| Below 40% | Extra-care day | Patience, reassurance or personal space may be especially helpful. |

**[NOTES]** *"A lower score should always be followed by practical guidance so
that the user knows what to do with the information."*

**[REC]** Make that a lint rule, not an intention: every band must carry a
non-empty action string, asserted in `content-rules.test.ts`.

### 3.3 Confidence wording

**[NOTES]** Before enough feedback has been collected the wording differs from
the wording used afterwards; the demo should communicate that the score becomes
more accurate and individual to the couple as they continue using cyncd.

**[OPEN]** The notes describe this transition but the two wordings were not
captured in the document. Needs the exact copy.

### 3.4 Placement

| Screen | What appears |
|---|---|
| **Her Home** | Smaller preview beneath her main daily insight: percentage, status, one line of meaning, button to open the full score. Her cycle information stays separate from the shared score. |
| **His Home** | The same preview, focused on practical relationship meaning rather than her private cycle information. |
| **Partner** | The **full** score. Large percentage, category, short explanation, a completed-action button, expandable explanation, sharing option, and recent Score history. |
| **Plan** | Upcoming *predicted* score to help choose better days — "High-connection day", "Good day for a date", "Keep plans flexible", "Extra-care day", "Good day for an important conversation". Must support the calendar without taking it over. |
| **Reflect** | End of day, asks about the day. A slider or five simple options, a few seconds to complete. Answers influence future scores. |

**[NOTES]** Both partners see the same overall score; their private responses
stay separate unless they choose to share them.

### 3.5 Sharing

**[NOTES]** Users can generate a premium branded card shareable to Instagram
Stories, WhatsApp, TikTok and elsewhere.

**[REC]** `expo-sharing` plus an off-screen view captured with
`react-native-view-shot`. The card must contain only the score, the band label
and branding — never cycle data. Worth an explicit test.

### 3.6 Product rule

**[NOTES]**, quoted in full because it governs everything above:

> The cyncd Score measures the connection conditions surrounding the couple
> that day. It does not measure the quality, health or future of their
> relationship. Every score must lead to understanding and a useful action. It
> should never create blame, fear or judgement.

## 4. Forecast / Track — Her only

**[NOTES]** *"This is the additional screen Her side needs. It should be her
central place for tracking + understanding her forecast."*

### 4.1 Forecast

Current menstrual cycle phase · cycle day · predicted next period · 7-day
forecast · button to a monthly/calendar view · mood outlook · energy outlook ·
symptoms and patterns · upcoming changes · cyncd colour coding across ·
tap a day to understand it in more detail.

### 4.2 Track / Log

Period start and end · flow · mood · energy · pain and cramps · sleep · stress ·
physical symptoms · emotional symptoms · libido · optional notes.

**[NOTES]** She must also be able to correct previous information.

### 4.3 Constraint

**[NOTES]** *"This needs to be a genuinely useful tracker without turning cyncd
into a traditional period app. Her information powers cyncd"* — and is
translated across the rest of the app.

**[REC]** This is the largest single build in the spec and the only part that
requires a real prediction model over logged data rather than a scripted week.
It is also the input the Score depends on, which makes sequencing awkward — see
§9.

## 5. Changed screens

### 5.1 Today — both users

**[NOTES]** Remains the main Home screen for both. The main daily cyncd quote
appears prominently for both. Each side gets: the daily quote, gentle reminders
about app setup notified at the top of the screen, a relevant relationship
insight, and a simple suggestion or action. Her information can be slightly
more personal because cyncd works directly from her tracked information.

> **IMPORTANT** — I want these screens to visually match. They are looking at
> the same day and the same relationship, just from their individual
> perspective.

**Gap from today's build:** the Score preview is new; the "gentle reminders"
top-of-screen slot is new and is distinct from the existing notification
banner. Otherwise Today is close to correct already.

### 5.2 Partner — both users, shared space

**[NOTES]** *"Rather than cyncd automatically showing everything about Her to
Him, this gives both users control over what they choose to share."*

- **Shared with me** — things their partner chose to share: feelings, notes and
  messages, relevant insights, important information, needs and preferences,
  things they want their partner to understand.
- **Shared by me** — what I chose to share, ability to edit or remove, choose
  what stays private.

**[NOTES]** She could share a particular cyncd insight from her Forecast. He can
share information back. This must **not** become a messaging replacement — it is
a controlled bridge between two cyncd experiences, and the data feeds their
prediction over time.

**Gap from today's build:** this is an inversion. The app currently *derives*
what he sees from her day, gated by a single global `sharing.paused` boolean.
The spec wants **per-item, user-initiated sharing with edit and revoke**. The
boolean is replaced by a collection.

### 5.3 Plan — both users

**[NOTES]** Three stacked sections:

1. **Shared sticky-note calendar** — visual and clean, both users contribute.
   Plans, dates, saved activities, important events, cyncd colour indicators,
   suggested good days, sticky notes from either partner. Icons for special
   events in the top-right corner of a date. Ability to link Google / Apple /
   Outlook calendars.
2. **cyncd suggestions** — timing-based, e.g. *"Saturday — Date Night"*. Actions:
   Save · Add to Calendar · Share.
3. **Ask cyncd** — a simple chatbox, *"directly inside Plan rather than making
   another screen"*. Answers using Forecast + shared information + existing
   plans + previous feedback.

**Gap from today's build:** the calendar is entirely new. The assistant exists
but lives in the wrong place — it is currently a global sheet opened from chips
on Today, and the spec puts a chatbox inside Plan.

**[OPEN]** Does the chip-driven coach on Today survive alongside the Plan
chatbox, or is it replaced by it? The notes describe the Plan chatbox and are
silent on the Today chips.

### 5.4 Reflect — both users

**[NOTES]** *"I want this to feel much more natural."* cyncd opens with something
simple and the user just types. cyncd understands the information, responds
appropriately, and logs useful signals in the background. It may ask **one**
relevant follow-up where useful *"rather than presenting ten questions."*

Why the screen exists:

> Prediction → Real experience → Reflection → cyncd learns → Future guidance
> improves

**Gap from today's build:** currently a slider plus mood chips plus a timeline.
The spec wants free-text conversation. Note the tension with §3.4, which says
the Score's end-of-day question should be *"a slider or five simple options"* —
so Reflect is conversational **and** carries one quick structured question.

### 5.5 Welcome / opening screen

**[NOTES]** A small transition page to make opening cyncd feel intentional
rather than transactional. Shows the wordmark line — *"Stay in cyncd. Know the
day together."* — a Continue affordance, and their current quote (e.g. *"A
connection day / Thoughtful gestures may go a long way today."*). Lasts around
two seconds before revealing the Home screen.

**[OPEN]** Once after onboarding, or on every app open? The section is titled
"Welcome Screen (after onboarding)" but the stated purpose — *"to make opening
cyncd feel intentional"* — reads as every launch. These are different builds:
the first is one more `Flow` state, the second is app-lifecycle behaviour that
has to handle backgrounding.

## 6. Navigation

**[NOTES]**

| Her | Him |
|---|---|
| TODAY — daily cyncd insight | TODAY — daily cyncd insight |
| **FORECAST** — her cycle, symptoms, tracking and future outlook | — |
| PARTNER — what we choose to share with each other | PARTNER — what we choose to share with each other |
| PLAN — our calendar, suggestions and planning AI | PLAN — our calendar, suggestions and planning AI |
| REFLECT — tell cyncd what actually happened | REFLECT — tell cyncd what actually happened |

**Gap from today's build:** `src/content/index.ts` exports one flat `TABS`
array used by both roles. It becomes a function of role, and `TabId` gains
`'forecast'`. The `TabBar` component already keys off `TabId`, so the change is
contained — but `openNotification()` returns a `TabId` to deep-link to, and
must never return `'forecast'` for him.

## 7. Data model changes

Beyond the ported `CyncdState`:

| Addition | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle log | Per-day entries covering §4.2 | Hers, private, editable retroactively |
| Cycle prediction | Phase, cycle day, predicted next period, 7-day outlook | Derived from the log |
| Score | Daily percentage + band + action + history | Derived; history is shared |
| Shared items | `{ id, author, kind, body, sharedAt, revoked }` | Replaces the `sharing.paused` boolean |
| Calendar entries | `{ id, date, title, kind, author, icon? }` | Both authors; plus external-calendar links |
| Reflection transcript | Free text + extracted signals + the one structured answer | Replaces `{ accuracy, mood }` |
| Per-user feedback | Separate per partner, private by default | Feeds the Score's 10% and 20% components |

`SCHEMA_VERSION` must be bumped; `reconcile()` in `src/state/persistence.ts`
needs guards for every new collection. Note that `reconcile` currently casts
`raw.flow` to the union **without validating it** — worth fixing in the same
pass, since this spec adds `Flow` states.

## 8. Where AI is, and is not

The notes settle a question that was previously open:

| Feature | Implementation |
|---|---|
| cyncd Score | **Rule-based.** Explicitly *"does not require advanced AI"* |
| Forecast prediction | **Statistical**, over her logged data |
| Daily quote / insight / suggestion | **Authored content**, selected by phase and score |
| **Reflect** | **Model.** Free-text in, understanding + one follow-up out |
| **Ask cyncd** (in Plan) | **Model.** Grounded in Forecast + shared info + plans + feedback |

Two model surfaces, both conversational, both needing the guardrails in §2 at
runtime rather than only at build time. Everything else is deterministic — which
is cheaper, testable, and demonstrable without a network call.

## 9. Privacy rules

**[NOTES]** Both partners see the shared Score, but the partner must **not**
automatically see: her cycle day, her menstrual phase, symptoms she has logged,
private notes, her individual feedback, or any information she has not chosen
to share.

> The score should translate the relevant information into relationship
> guidance without exposing private health or cycle data.

**[REC]** This is the single most testable requirement in the document and the
most damaging to get wrong. Enforce it as a boundary, not a convention:

1. Partner-facing state is derived through one function. Nothing else may read
   the cycle log.
2. A test asserts that no partner-facing string contains cycle vocabulary, on
   every day of the scripted week and every score band.
3. A test asserts the share card contains no cycle data.

## 10. Suggested build order

Sequenced so each stage is demonstrable on its own. **[REC]** throughout.

| Stage | Work | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Rescope the content lint (§2) | Blocks all new content |
| **1** | cyncd Score: calculation, bands, copy, Today preview, full Partner view | Headline feature, rule-based, no new data model, visible immediately |
| **2** | Navigation asymmetry + Forecast **read-only** (scripted phase data) | Unblocks the Her/Him split without waiting for the tracker |
| **3** | Track/Log + real prediction; Score switches to real inputs | The big one; Score already exists to consume it |
| **4** | Partner rebuild: per-item sharing, edit, revoke | Depends on there being insights worth sharing |
| **5** | Plan: calendar, then Ask cyncd | Calendar is independent; the assistant needs §3–4 for grounding |
| **6** | Reflect: conversational rewrite | Needs the model surface and the guardrails proven |
| **7** | Welcome screen, share card | Polish; both are small and self-contained |

Stage 1 alone would demo well and is perhaps a week of work. Stage 3 is the
long pole.

## 11. Open questions

1. **Welcome screen** — once after onboarding, or every app open? (§5.5)
2. **Confidence wording** — the exact before/after copy for the Score's
   maturity transition was not captured. (§3.3)
3. **Does the Today coach survive?** The spec puts a chatbox in Plan and is
   silent on the existing chips. (§5.3)
4. **Does the scripted 7-day week survive?** `src/content/days.ts` carries 14
   verbatim spec-mandated sentences locked by test. This spec implies content
   driven by her actual phase rather than a fixed script. If the script goes,
   so does the verbatim lock — and that was a deliberate guarantee worth
   replacing rather than dropping.
5. **Him's onboarding** — he now has a Score, a shared space and a calendar.
   Are his four onboarding questions still the right four?
6. **Score history retention** — "recent cyncd Score history" is required on the
   Partner screen. How far back, and does it survive a partner leaving?
